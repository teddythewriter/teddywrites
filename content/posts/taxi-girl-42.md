It was almost time for the sun to rise. As I got up I remembered that I had promised her that I would come to meet her. I checked my messages and saw her message,

"Be there at 5 pm, OK?"

I replied with a simple okay.

It wasn't the first time I was meeting her, but it was the first time I'm meeting her alone. They say time passes quickly when you're nervous. Although it's a total bullshit theory with no realism, once you feel it, you feel it. As I was walking towards the café I saw her familiar face.

"Hi, I didn't know you were punctual." She greeted me with a smile.

"Neither did I," I muttered. I think it was barely audible.

"What? Did you say something?" She asked.

"Nope, nothing," I replied.

She was the definition of a 9 if I'm being honest. No tenner cause, nobody is a 10. I'm a 3 or 4 on a good day and that's literally 2 and a half of me there, so people were googling their eyes towards us.

"So, listen. I know this is sudden and out of nowhere, but I have to give you this." She told me as she handed me a book.

It was her way of returning a favor and I really liked that.

Even though we were talking about whatever we could conjure up, while listening to her voice, I couldn't get my eyes off of her eyes and her smile. I could fall over them again and again for no reason. Those scintillating eyes and the brightest of smiles held me captive while I listened to her talk about her passions, dreams, and hopes. I couldn't do anything else but smile like an idiot.

"So that's pretty much about it. What about you? What do you think?" She stopped and asked me.

"Well, nothing. I don't want to think about anything because this is not the moment for it." I replied to her in a dreamy state.

"I must go now. It's already been 2 hours and I gotta go home before it's 9 pm, thank you for coming." She said as I held the door for her.

"Until next time then?" I asked hopefully.

"Until next time." She told me as she got in her cab. I watched it take her away From me.

A love that's unattainable, a love that's so controversial in so many aspects of society.\
Oh, dear boy, you've gone and done it to yourself again. But this time, this time it will be much harder because this one is as close as it gets.

I hummed John Mayer's New light while I walked to the bus station.

"But if you give me just one night\
You're gonna see me in a new light\
Yeah, if you give me just one night\
To meet you underneath the moonlight\
Oh, I want a take two, I wanna breakthrough\
I wanna know the real thing about you\
So I can see you in a new light"

It was serene.
